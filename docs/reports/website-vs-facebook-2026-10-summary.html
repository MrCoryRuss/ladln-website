Key points

  • The website is effectively one page. 9 out of 10 visits never leave the homepage. Average visit is 1.1 minutes. The Students page was opened once in seven weeks.
  • People stop a third of the way down. Everyone sees the banner and the scholarship stats. Only 17% scroll far enough to reach the graduates' stories and photos. New photos added where they sit today would be seen by about 13 people a month.
  • Facebook is our front door. It is the top known source of website traffic, and the "See our latest on Facebook" card is the most clicked thing on the homepage after the menu.
  • Facebook is growing fast. Reach up 40%, page visits up 44%, interactions up 43% over the prior month. 77% of people seeing our posts do not follow us yet; Facebook is introducing us to new people every day. The website cannot do that.
  • The website does one thing well: Donate. Donate buttons are the most clicked element on the site and five visitors reached the Zeffy checkout. Keep that path clean and fast.
  • Same photo, 100x the audience. A typical post reached 1,000 to 5,800 people in a day. The website already republishes our Facebook feed automatically, so one upload lands in both places.

Recommendations

Do this (low effort, real payoff)

  • Post student and graduate photos to Facebook first. The website picks them up for free.
  • Move a short "Meet our graduates" strip to just under the stats banner. Takes the photos from 17% of visitors to 100%. About one hour of work, one time.
  • Fix the Facebook feed box on phones. It errors on some devices and a third of visitors are on mobile.
  • Tag links in emails and newsletters so we can see which outreach drives visits. 60% of traffic currently has no known source.
  • Keep Donate and Membership one click from the top. Already true; protect it.

Hold off (effort will not be seen)

  • A large photo gallery on the website. The audience for it is on Facebook, not here.
  • More sections on the homepage. People already stop a third of the way down.
  • Weekly website content updates. With 2 to 3 visits a day, nobody is checking back for news. 1,100 Facebook followers are.
  • Search engine (SEO) investment. Google sends about 2 visitors a week.
